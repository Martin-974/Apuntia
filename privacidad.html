Privacy Policy

“Apuntia” iPhone app · Effective November 14, 2026

Summary

Apuntia does not collect, send or share any personal data. Your notes and decks stay on your iPhone.

Notes, photos and PDFs

Photos, PDFs and text you add are read on your iPhone to extract the text. That text and your decks are stored only on your iPhone. There are no accounts or servers.

Artificial intelligence

Summaries, flashcards and quizzes are created with Apple Intelligence right on your iPhone. Your notes are never sent to the internet or to third parties.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan the documents you choose.

Subscriptions

Payments for Apuntia Pro are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

You can delete decks and cards in the app. Deleting the app removes all its data from your iPhone.
